Varieties packed for international transit

Each lot is hand-picked, size-graded, and packed in ventilated cartons. Box weights follow the buyer’s market — typically 3 kg, 3.5 kg, 5 kg, or a custom size.

Banganapalli

Sweet, low-fibre fruit from Andhra Pradesh. Holds well on sea and air freight to the USA, UK, and Gulf.

  • Origin — Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Packing — 3 kg, 3.5 kg, 5 kg, or custom

Alphonso

Rich, creamy “king of mangoes” from Maharashtra. Strong demand in UK, Europe, and Asia retail.

  • Origin — Maharashtra, India
  • Packing — 3 kg, 3.5 kg, 5 kg, or custom

Rasallu

Juicy Andhra fruit with a strong aroma. Suited to USA, UK, Europe, and Asia programmes.

  • Origin — Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Packing — 3 kg, 3.5 kg, 5 kg, or custom

Totapuri

Tangy, long-shaped fruit from South India. Mainly packed for pulp, juice, and beverage plants.

  • Origin — South India
  • Packing — 3 kg, 3.5 kg, 5 kg, or custom

Kesar

Intense aroma and saffron flesh from Gujarat. Favoured by retail buyers in Canada, Australia, and the UK.

  • Origin — Gujarat, India
  • Packing — 3 kg, 3.5 kg, 5 kg, or custom

USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe & Asia
