Our story

A KR Puram shop, not a real-estate brochure.

Alfa Garden is the local name for Daily Cart 24/7’s fruit and vegetable retail. The company was incorporated on 9 January 2019 at ROC Bangalore. The registered address is inside Alpha Garden, Kodigehalli — that is why the domain is alfa-garden.com.
