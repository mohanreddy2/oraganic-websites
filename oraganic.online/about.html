Our story

A market stall that grew into a weekly box.

Oraganic started by buying surplus from growers who already farmed without synthetic shortcuts. The idea is still the same: pay farms fairly, pack what is actually ripe, and keep the story attached to the food.

How we choose farms

We look for living soil, crop rotation, and growers who can tell us what they used — and what they refused to use. We visit when we can, and we publish the farm name on every box card.

What we will not do

We do not blend anonymous produce to hit a price. We do not spray “fresh” perfume on tired greens. If a crop is late, the box changes. That is the point of seasonal food.
