Toolkit

Five tools. One morning habit.

Each tool is meant to answer one question quickly. No social feed. No badges.

Field Scout

Upload a crop photo. Receive likely causes ranked by how common they are in organic systems, plus what to check next in the row.

Planting Calendar

Set your district and crop list. Get a short window for sow, transplant, and rest — updated when weather shifts.

Soil Notes

Keep pH, texture, and amendment history in one place. The assistant only suggests what matches your last notes.

Organic Guard

Type an input name. See a caution if it is commonly restricted, plus questions to ask your certifier.

Yield Outlook

A rough harvest range from plot size, variety, and recent stress flags — useful for CSA planning, not a guarantee.

Grower log

A dated diary you own. Export as a simple text file whenever you want a backup.
