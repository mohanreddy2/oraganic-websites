Compliance

Country-specific phytosanitary treatment

Mango export from India must match the importing country’s plant quarantine protocol. We pack and treat lots to these published standards.

Country Required treatment Treatment standard Box / labelling notes
USA, Australia, Malaysia Irradiation Minimum absorbed dose 400 Gy; maximum 1000 Gy Vent holes covered. Cartons about 370×275×90 mm up to 3.5 kg, or 365×265×110 mm for 3.5–4.2 kg. Marks: Product of India, fruit type, “Treated by Irradiation”, PUC, PHC, TFC, TIN, packing and treatment dates, lot number.
New Zealand Vapour heat treatment 48°C for 20 minutes at 95% relative humidity Mesh netting compulsory. Product of India and fruit type on the box.
Japan Vapour heat treatment Innermost pulp 47.5°C for 20 minutes or more Mesh netting compulsory. Mark “For Japan”. Non-laminated CFB may need pesticide-residue testing.
South Korea Vapour heat treatment 47.5°C for 20 minutes at 95% RH Mesh netting compulsory. Mark “For Korea”.
Russia, Brunei, Vietnam, Mauritius Vapour heat treatment 47.5–48°C for 20 minutes at 95% RH Mesh netting compulsory. Product of India and fruit type.
EU, Iran, China Hot water treatment 48°C for 60 minutes (fruit below 500 g) Mesh netting optional. Product of India and fruit type.
UK, Canada, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Singapore, Nepal Phytosanitary inspection Not a heat or irradiation protocol Pack to destination norms. Mesh netting optional. Product of India and fruit type.

Source: MSAMB and APPPC RSPM No. 11 (FAO). Confirm the current protocol with Plant Quarantine / APEDA before each season.
