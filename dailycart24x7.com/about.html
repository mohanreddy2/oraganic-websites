About Daily Cart 24/7

Daily Cart 24/7 Private Limited was incorporated in India on 9 January 2019 (CIN U52202KA2019PTC128429). We trade fresh fruit for export and also operate the DailyCart local commerce app.

Mango export

Our export desk sources Banganapalli, Totapuri, Neelam, and Alphonso from the Chittoor growing belt and allied orchards. We pack to destination phytosanitary rules and ship by air or reefer container.

Growing region

Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh, is a major mango district. Fruit from this belt is known for firm flesh and flavour suited to export packing and Gulf, ASEAN, and EU buyers.
